BOM vs Actual Audit

Find designs whose recipe (BOM) doesn't match what production actually used.
For every posted Work Order in the selected date range, this page compares BOM × WO qty (what the design says you should use) against materials actually issued from store. Three things can be wrong:
Wrong Qty BOM says 10/unit, floor issued 7/unit → fix the BOM number.
Wrong Item BOM listed an item that was never issued → wrong material in BOM.
Missing from BOM Floor used something the BOM doesn't list → add it to the BOM.
Why this matters: a wrong BOM makes auto-PR over- or under-purchase. Fix the worst-drifting designs first.
